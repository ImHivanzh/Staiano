# Architectural finish textures

The six JPG textures in `oak/` and `stone/` are original, unmodified 1K files downloaded from Poly Haven. They are dedicated to the public domain under **CC0 1.0 Universal**. The textures may be used and redistributed, including within commercial software.

License: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Poly Haven asset license: https://polyhaven.com/license

## Sources

- `oak`: **Wood Floor**, Dimitrios Savva. https://polyhaven.com/a/wood_floor
- `stone`: **Marble 01**, Rob Tuytel. https://polyhaven.com/a/marble_01

Each material includes diffuse/base color, OpenGL normal and roughness maps. The provider reports a repeat width of approximately **1.7 m** for Wood Floor and **1.5 m** for Marble 01. Exact API dimensions, download URLs, retrieval dates, file sizes, source MD5 and local SHA-256 checksums are recorded in `manifest.json`.

These materials illustrate architectural finishes. They do not specify actual installed flooring or an HVAC manufacturer product. All rendering assets are served locally; the running configurator does not call Poly Haven or require a network connection to that service.

No source preview render, logo, avatar or website copy is included. Credit is retained here for provenance although CC0 does not require attribution.
